# Academy Tech Kids

**Module:** 01 - Foundations

**Week:** 01

**Theme:** Welcome to Academy Tech Kids

**Recommended Age:** 12–14 years

**Estimated Duration:** 90 minutes

**Difficulty:** Beginner

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson students should be able to:

- Understand the purpose of Academy Tech Kids.
- Meet their classmates and mentor.
- Feel excited about the learning journey.
- Recognize that technology is present in everyday life.
- Define one personal learning goal.

---

# Competencies

- Communication
- Curiosity
- Teamwork
- Respect
- Self-awareness

---

# Required Materials

- Whiteboard
- Markers
- Notebook
- Pencil
- Internet connection (optional)

---

# Lesson Timeline

## Part 1 – Welcome (10 minutes)

Welcome every student personally.

Questions:

- What is your name?
- What do you enjoy doing?
- What would you like to learn?

**Goal**

Create a safe and welcoming environment.

---

## Part 2 – What is Academy Tech Kids? (15 minutes)

Explain the purpose of the project.

Suggested introduction:

> "Academy Tech Kids is not just a technology course.
> It is a place where we learn how to think, solve problems, work together and build amazing things."

Explain that nobody starts knowing everything.

Learning is a journey.

---

## Part 3 – Icebreaker (15 minutes)

Each student introduces themselves.

Suggested format:

- My name is...
- I am ... years old.
- My favorite hobby is...
- One dream I have...
- One thing I want to learn...

---

## Part 4 – Where is Technology? (20 minutes)

Ask:

> "Where do you see technology every day?"

Allow students to answer first.

Examples:

- Smartphones
- Television
- Video games
- Cars
- Hospitals
- Schools
- Traffic lights
- Banks

Explain that technology is much more than computers.

---

## Part 5 – Practical Activity (20 minutes)

Activity:

Draw your daily routine.

From the moment you wake up until bedtime.

Now identify every technology you used during the day.

Discuss the results together.

---

# Weekly Mission

Talk to a family member.

Ask:

> "What is the biggest technological change you have seen during your lifetime?"

Write down the answer.

Be ready to share it next week.

---

# Reflection Questions

- What surprised you today?
- What technology do you use the most?
- What would you like to learn during this project?

---

# Success Criteria

Students can:

- Explain what Academy Tech Kids is.
- Identify technology in everyday life.
- Define one personal learning goal.
