# Academy Tech Kids

**Module:** 01 - Foundations

**Week:** 02

**Theme:** Learning is a Skill

**Recommended Age:** 12–14 years

**Estimated Duration:** 90 minutes

**Difficulty:** Beginner

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson students should be able to:

- Understand that everyone can learn.
- Recognize that mistakes are part of learning.
- Develop a simple study routine.
- Discover the concept of an algorithm through everyday activities.

# Lesson Timeline

## Part 1 – Weekly Review (15 minutes)

Students share the results of the weekly mission.

Questions:

- What did your family member tell you?
- What surprised you?

Discuss the different answers.

---

## Part 2 – Learning Never Stops (15 minutes)

Ask:

- Were you born knowing how to walk?
- Were you born knowing how to read?
- Were you born knowing mathematics?

Students will answer "No."

Explain:

Everything we know today was learned.

Technology is no different.

---

## Part 3 – How the Brain Learns (15 minutes)

Explain using simple language.

Suggested explanation:

Every time we learn something new, our brain creates new connections.

The more we practice, the stronger those connections become.

Learning takes time.

Making mistakes is part of the learning process.

---

## Part 4 – Practical Activity (25 minutes)

Activity:

Draw the route from your home to school.

Now imagine explaining this route to a robot.

Students should describe every step.

Example:

- Walk forward.
- Turn right.
- Cross the street.
- Continue for 100 meters.

After the activity explain:

> "A sequence of instructions like this is called an algorithm."

---

## Part 5 – Discussion (10 minutes)

Question:

> "Can computers think by themselves?"

Guide the discussion.

Explain that computers execute instructions created by people.

---

# Weekly Mission

Organize your study space.

It can be:

- A desk
- A table
- A quiet corner

Write down three things that help you study better.

---

# Reflection Questions

- What was the most interesting part of today's lesson?
- Why do mistakes help us learn?
- What is an algorithm?

---

# Success Criteria

Students can:

- Explain that learning requires practice.
- Describe an algorithm using their own words.
- Organize a simple study environment.
